# CSCI 344 SLO Assessment Draft

## Recommended Artifact

- **Course:** CSCI 344
- **Primary SLO:** **SLO 9**
- **Level:** **Mastery**
- **Artifact:** the **implemented final project milestone**

This is the strongest fit because the revised final project now asks students to:

1. define app requirements and endpoints
2. implement login/profile behavior using starter auth code
3. build CRUD functionality
4. integrate third-party components
5. deliver a coherent working SPA

## Recommended Measurable Criteria

Use these 5 implementation dimensions for scoring:

1. login/profile workflow works correctly
2. at least 2 endpoints are implemented and used appropriately
3. CRUD functionality works for the primary resource
4. at least 2 third-party components are integrated meaningfully
5. the app is coherent, usable, and complete enough to demonstrate the intended workflow

## Recommended Thresholds

These are intentionally conservative so the **minimum goal** is realistic and easier to meet:

- **Minimum goal:** 75% of students will meet **at least 4 of the 5** implementation criteria above on the final project.
- **Stretch goal:** 90% of students will meet **at least 4 of the 5** implementation criteria above on the final project.

## Suggested Reporting Language

### Version to use if the goal is met

**Mastery**  
**CSCI 344 - SLO 9**

- **Goal:** 75% of students will meet at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria: login/profile workflow, endpoint integration, CRUD functionality, third-party component integration, and overall app coherence/usability.
- **Stretch:** 90% of students will meet at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria.

**Met**

`[XX]%` of students met at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria.

Students demonstrated SLO 9 by implementing a focused single-page application that used their own API design, supported user-specific behavior, implemented CRUD operations, and integrated third-party components into a working computer-based solution.

### Version to use if the goal is not met

**Mastery**  
**CSCI 344 - SLO 9**

- **Goal:** 75% of students will meet at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria: login/profile workflow, endpoint integration, CRUD functionality, third-party component integration, and overall app coherence/usability.
- **Stretch:** 90% of students will meet at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria.

**Unmet**

`[XX]%` of students met at least 4 of the 5 final project implementation criteria.

Students were generally able to define project scope and build portions of the final application, but a substantial number of projects fell short in one or more of the following areas: user-specific workflow, full CRUD behavior, or meaningful integration of third-party components.

**Operational plan:** In the next offering of the course, add one intermediate graded checkpoint focused specifically on implementation readiness: endpoint review, starter-auth integration, and CRUD planning. Also add a second checkpoint requiring a working prototype with at least one completed third-party component before the final submission.

## Backup Framing

If you decide later that the **design milestone** is the better artifact, use **SLO 1** instead.

Suggested version:

- **SLO:** SLO 1
- **Level:** Reinforcing
- **Artifact:** Stage 1 final project planning and wireframing milestone
- **Minimum goal:** 80% of students will submit a focused project concept, at least 2 endpoints, and wireframes that clearly map the primary user flow.
- **Stretch goal:** 90% of students will submit all of those elements at a strong level.

This backup is useful if you want to assess problem analysis and computing requirements rather than final implementation quality.

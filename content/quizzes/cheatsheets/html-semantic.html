HTML Semantic Tags Cheatsheet

Semantic HTML tags clearly describe their purpose and help organize content into logical sections. They improve accessibility, SEO, and code readability.

Tag Examples Notes
<header>
  • <header>Site title and logo</header>
  • Top section of page (site title, logo, navigation)
<nav>
  • <nav>Navigation links</nav>
  • Navigation section (menu, links)
<main>
  • <main>Main content</main>
  • Main content area (should appear once per page)
<article>
  • <article>Blog post or article</article>
  • Self-contained content (blog post, news article)
<section>
  • <section>Thematic content group</section>
  • Thematic grouping of content
<aside>
  • <aside>Sidebar content</aside>
  • Sidebar content (related but separate from main content)
<footer>
  • <footer>Copyright and links</footer>
  • Bottom section (copyright, contact info, links)
